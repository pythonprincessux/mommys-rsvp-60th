Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through CelebrateSharima.com is collected and used in connection with Sharima's 60th Birthday Gala.

Information We Collect

When you RSVP, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, phone number, attendance status, number of guests, meal selection, hotel planning information, song requests, notes, and SMS consent preference.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the website is used to coordinate Sharima's 60th Birthday Gala, manage RSVPs, plan guest counts and meals, provide hotel and venue information, and communicate event-related updates.

SMS and Mobile Information

Mobile information will not be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Guests who voluntarily opt in to SMS messaging may receive event-related text messages, including hotel booking reminders, RSVP updates, event countdown reminders, venue information, arrival information, and other important updates related to Sharima's 60th Birthday Gala.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of future text messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Service Providers

We may use service providers to operate the RSVP and event communication systems, including website hosting, database, and messaging providers. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide those services.

Mobile information and SMS consent information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Your Choices

Providing a mobile phone number and consenting to SMS communications is optional. You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.

Data Use

Guest information is used only for purposes reasonably related to planning, administering, and communicating information about Sharima's 60th Birthday Gala.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or event communications may be directed to the event host using the contact information provided with the event invitation.
